\newcommand{\CalibText}{Table~\ref{tab:calib} gives the run-level error of every test. From 200 pseudo-donors up, pseudobulk and LCP made false calls in every run, and LCPd and single-split XDS in 14--32\% of runs. XDS-M made at least one false call in 3--8\% of runs from 100 pseudo-donors up (\RtwoPctAllXDSM{} at full size) and stayed within the frozen bound in \XDSMholdsLarge{} subtype--size and blood settings, failing only ADAM\_GPNMB at 400 pseudo-donors. The bound rejects an exactly calibrated method in about 3\% of settings, so one narrow failure is compatible with nominal error, but the data also allow a true rate of up to about 10\%. Below 100 donors XDS-M's normal reference failed (49\% of runs at 25 pseudo-donors), and KX, which is within bound from 200 donors on the donor-centred null, failed at small sizes and on the pooled null in ADAM\_GPNMB (\RfKXpoolThree{} of 100 runs).

\emph{Small cohorts and permutation resolution.} With $10^5$ permutations the smallest attainable $p$-value ($10^{-5}$) exceeds the first BH threshold among 89{,}000 pairs ($5.7\times10^{-7}$), so a permutation test can make a call only when many pairs tie at the floor, and the absence of false calls in our earlier small-cohort runs said little. With $2\times10^6$ permutations (REV4; at least $m/q$ for $m$ pairs at level $q$), LCP made false calls in \RfLCPTwentyFive{}, \RfLCPFifty{} and \RfLCPHundred{} runs at 25, 50 and 100 pseudo-donors and LCPd in \RfLCPdTwentyFive{}, \RfLCPdFifty{} and \RfLCPdHundred, whereas XDS with permutation $p$-values made false calls in \RfXDSpermTwentyFive{}, \RfXDSpermFifty{} and \RfXDSpermHundred{} runs (within the bound in \RfXDSpermTwentyFiveHolds, \RfXDSpermFiftyHolds{} and \RfXDSpermHundredHolds{} of three subtypes; Appendix Table~\ref{tab:R1}). Permutation-based XDS was the only test we found calibrated below 100 donors, at low power (Section~\ref{sec:sim}).

\emph{Batches.} On the brain-bank-stratified null XDS-M made false calls in \RfBankXDSM{} runs, outside the bound in two subtypes, and every other test failed in at least one subtype (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:R2}). These pseudo-donors are not exactly null: donor-centring shrinks few-cell donors' effects toward the overall mean, so part of each bank's mean survives (median shift \RfBankShift{} SD over genes), which is real covariance shared by the pseudo-donors of a bank. Centring the design-standardized values within bank before forming the products (XDS-Mb; post hoc, REV4 Amendment~2) reduced the runs with a call to \RfBankXDSMb{}, within the bound in \RfBankXDSMbHolds{} subtypes, whereas bank-centred pseudobulk still made \RfBankPBb{} false calls per run. Known batches should be centred out, as AD groups are in Section~\ref{sec:dc}.}
\newcommand{\RecText}{With about 100 donors or more, XDS-M was the most reliable test we found: 3--8\% of runs with any false call across cohort sizes and tissues, for genes above about 0.1 UMI per cell and with known batches centred out, at some cost in power (\XDSMLpow{} against 0.98 for LCP at 200 simulated donors with a median of 20 cells).
