\newcommand{\PlanSixText}{\paragraph{Planted signal, depth heterogeneity and confounders.} Three further nulls test what the earlier ones cannot (Table~\ref{tab:plansix}). \emph{Planted signal} (pre-specified): we planted four ten-gene modules with between-donor correlations 0.1--0.4 (180 true pairs) into the donor-centred null, together with independent between-donor variation of every gene at the observed median coefficient of variation (\SpS). At full cohort size \SpPBfdr{} of pseudobulk's discoveries were false. XDS-M kept the false discovery proportion at \SpXfdr{} with power close to pseudobulk's (\SpXpow{} vs.\ \SpPBpow), and its cross-split correlation XDS-r recovered the planted correlations with mean bias at most \SpXrBias, whereas pseudobulk correlations were attenuated by \SpPBrBias. \emph{Donor-specific depth distributions} (pre-specified): Lemma~\ref{lem:coupling}(ii) predicts a bias that pooled nulls cannot contain. In a depth-matched null, where each pseudo-donor inherits its real donor's depth distribution, XDS-M made false calls in \DmXany{} of 20 runs. Residualizing each half on its own design summaries ($\log\hat r^h_d$, $\log n^h_d$; XDS-Mh, post hoc, the third variant we tried) removed them (\MhDany{} runs), passed every other null in Table~\ref{tab:plansix} without losing power, and kept \MhKeepPsy{} of XDS-M's real-donor calls in brain and \MhKeepOnek{} in blood; we recommend it. \emph{Confounders} (pre-specified, then post hoc): with 200 genes scaled by 1.2 in AD cases, adjusting the standardized values (XDS-Mc) left false calls in \CfXcany{} of 20 runs, because standardization makes a multiplicative effect depend on each donor's design; adjusting the half means first and standardizing second (XDS-Mca) removed them (\CfXcaany{} runs; type-I \CfXcatI). The reordering also resolves the sex control (\emph{XIST}--Y pairs called after adjustment: \CaXISTY{} of 7; Appendix~\ref{app:plan6}). Combining XDS-Mca with XDS-Mh held at run level but not per pair (type-I \HcTI).}

\newcommand{\LupusText}{\paragraph{Second disease: lupus.} In the lupus PBMC cohort of \citet{Perez2022} (162 SLE and 99 control donors in 24 multiplexed pools), analysed under a plan hashed before any expression was read, pseudobulk again made false calls under the donor-centred null (99{,}681 pairs, 20 reassignments): 16{,}631 per run in classical monocytes (median 1{,}005 cells per donor; type-I 0.29) and 3{,}472 in plasmacytoid dendritic cells (15 cells per donor; 0.17). XDS-M made 0.0 and 0.1 (type-I 0.051, 0.050). For SLE-versus-control differential co-expression, pseudobulk called 8{,}937 and 1{,}493 pairs per run on the label null, 22\% and 12\% of its real-donor calls, and XDS-Mgu none. Multiplexed pools are a genuine between-donor factor here: in a null that keeps each donor's libraries XDS-M made 48{,}661 false calls per run in monocytes, and conditioning on pool composition (post hoc) removed them (0.5 per run) with per-pair inflation (0.061--0.080), the same degrees-of-freedom problem as in Appendix~\ref{app:plan6}. Pool-adjusted XDS-Mgu held both nulls and still called 18{,}211 SLE-associated pairs in monocytes, including 487 of the 496 interferon-stimulated gene pairs, all stronger in SLE.}
