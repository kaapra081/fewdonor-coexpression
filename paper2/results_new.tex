\newcommand{\PlanSixText}{\paragraph{Planted signal, depth heterogeneity and confounders.} Three further nulls test what the earlier ones cannot (Table~\ref{tab:plansix}). \emph{Planted signal} (pre-specified): we planted four ten-gene modules with between-donor correlations 0.1--0.4 (180 true pairs) into the donor-centred null, together with independent between-donor variation of every gene at the observed median coefficient of variation (\SpS). At full cohort size \SpPBfdr{} of pseudobulk's discoveries were false. XDS-M kept the false discovery proportion at \SpXfdr{} with power \SpXpow{} (pseudobulk's \SpPBpow{} count mostly false discoveries; the calibrated KX reached \SpKXpow), and PBw held it only in Homeo\_CECR2 (\SpPBwfdr), and XDS-r recovered the planted correlations with mean bias at most \SpXrBias{} (RMSE 0.05--0.10), whereas pseudobulk correlations were attenuated by \SpPBrBias. Pseudobulk's ranking fails too: at an oracle threshold where the false discovery proportion is 0.05 it found \PeMatchPB{} of the planted pairs, against \PeMatchXlist{} for XDS-M (Plan 8, post hoc). \emph{Donor-specific depth distributions} (pre-specified): Lemma~\ref{lem:coupling}(ii) predicts a bias that pooled nulls cannot contain. In a depth-matched null, where each pseudo-donor inherits its real donor's depth distribution, XDS-M made false calls in \DmXany{} of 20 runs. Residualizing each half on its own design summaries ($\log\hat r^h_d$, $\log n^h_d$; XDS-Mh, post hoc, the third variant we tried) removed them (\MhDany{} runs), passed every other null in Table~\ref{tab:plansix} without losing power, and kept \MhKeepPsy{} of XDS-M's real-donor calls in brain and \MhKeepOnek{} in blood; pooled over its four nulls it made a call in 12 of 210 runs (5.7\%; exact 95\% CI 3.0--9.8\%). Checks run afterwards (Plan 8, post hoc, hashed before computation) were mixed: in the held-out lupus cohort XDS-Mh made a call in \PeLAh{} of 20 runs (monocytes, pDCs; one false call each; XDS-M \PeLAx), failing in pDCs, and on the brain-bank null it failed in two subtypes (\PeBh{} of 40 runs; XDS-M \PeBx) unless bank indicators were added to its half-design columns (XDS-Mhb: \PeBhb). Over five nulls it made a call in \PeHk{} of \PeHn{} runs (\PeHpct; \PeHci). \emph{Confounders} (pre-specified, then post hoc): with 200 genes scaled by 1.2 in AD cases, adjusting the standardized values (XDS-Mc) left false calls in \CfXcany{} of 20 runs, because standardization makes a multiplicative effect depend on each donor's design; adjusting the half means first and standardizing second (XDS-Mca) removed them (\CfXcaany{} runs; type-I among planted pairs \CfXcatI). The reordering also reduces the sex-control failure (\emph{XIST}--Y pairs called after adjustment: 6, 0, 6 of 7 under XDS-Mc, \CaXISTY{} under XDS-Mca; Appendix~\ref{app:plan6}). Combining XDS-Mca with XDS-Mh held at run level but not per pair (type-I \HcTI).}

\newcommand{\LupusText}{\paragraph{Second disease: lupus.} In the lupus PBMC cohort of \citet{Perez2022} (162 systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) and 99 control donors; 24 pools inferred from library--donor incidence; Appendix Table~\ref{tab:lupus}), analysed under a plan hashed (SHA-256) before any expression was read, pseudobulk again made false calls under the donor-centred null (99{,}681 pairs, 20 reassignments): 16{,}631 per run in classical monocytes (median 1{,}005 cells per donor; type-I 0.29) and 3{,}472 in plasmacytoid dendritic cells (15 cells per donor; 0.17), so the predicted larger error at fewer cells did not hold and cell number is not the only driver (weighting by cell number cut the monocyte calls to 410 per run). XDS-M made 0.0 and 0.1 (type-I 0.051, 0.050). For SLE-versus-control differential co-expression, pseudobulk called 8{,}937 and 1{,}493 pairs per run on the label null, 22\% and 12\% of its real-donor calls (SLE donors had more monocytes and fewer pDCs than controls), and XDS-Mgu none. Multiplexed pools are a genuine between-donor factor here: in a null that keeps each donor's libraries XDS-M made 48{,}661 false calls per run in monocytes, and conditioning on pool composition (post hoc) cut them to 0.5 per run, but 7 of 20 runs still had a call and per-pair type-I was 0.061--0.080, failing both rules (Appendix~\ref{app:plan6}). Pool-adjusted XDS-Mgu held both nulls and called 18{,}211 SLE-associated pairs in monocytes, including 487 of 496 interferon-stimulated gene pairs, all stronger in SLE.}
