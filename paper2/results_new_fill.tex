\newcommand{\PlanSixText}{\paragraph{Planted signal, depth heterogeneity and confounders.} Three further nulls test what the earlier ones cannot (Table~\ref{tab:plan6}). \emph{Planted signal} (pre-specified): we planted four ten-gene modules with between-donor correlations 0.1--0.4 (180 true pairs) into the donor-centred null, together with independent between-donor variation of every gene at the observed median coefficient of variation (\SpS). At full cohort size \SpPBfdr{} of pseudobulk's discoveries were false. XDS-M kept the false discovery proportion at \SpXfdr{} with power close to pseudobulk's (\SpXpow{} vs.\ \SpPBpow), and its cross-split correlation XDS-r recovered the planted correlations with mean bias at most \SpXrBias, whereas pseudobulk correlations were attenuated by \SpPBrBias. \emph{Donor-specific depth distributions} (pre-specified): Lemma~\ref{lem:coupling}(ii) predicts a bias that pooled nulls cannot contain. In a depth-matched null, where each pseudo-donor inherits its real donor's depth distribution (SD of $\log\hat r_d$ \DmSDthis, real donors \DmSDreal, plain null \DmSDplain), XDS-M made false calls in \DmXany{} of 20 runs. Residualizing each half on its own design summaries ($\log\hat r^h_d$, $\log n^h_d$; XDS-Mh, post hoc, the third variant we tried) removed them (\MhDany{} runs), stayed calibrated in the donor-centred null at full size and 200 donors and in the OneK1K null, kept power on planted signal, and kept \MhKeepPsy{} of XDS-M's real-donor calls in brain and \MhKeepOnek{} in blood; we recommend it. \emph{Confounders} (pre-specified, then post hoc): with 200 genes scaled by 1.2 in AD cases, adjusting the standardized values (XDS-Mc) left false calls in \CfXcany{} of 20 runs, because standardization makes a multiplicative effect depend on each donor's design; adjusting the half means first and standardizing second (XDS-Mca) removed them (\CfXcaany{} runs; type-I \CfXcatI). The reordering also resolves the sex control: XIST--Y pairs called after adjustment fell to \CaXISTY{} of 7, and were not called within either sex, whereas Y--Y pairs stayed significant within men, whose Y expression fell with age (Spearman \SexLOY), as expected from mosaic loss of chromosome Y. Combining XDS-Mca with XDS-Mh held at run level but not per pair (type-I \HcTI).}
