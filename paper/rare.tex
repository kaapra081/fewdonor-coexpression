\paragraph{A naturally rare supertype (analysis plan frozen before the run).} To test the setting where the leak should matter, we analysed SEA-AD Micro-PVM\_1 nuclei, which are rare in every donor: 79 donors with at least four nuclei, median 15 per donor (1{,}361 nuclei), no down-sampling. The decision rules were written and committed before any statistic was computed (repository \texttt{prereg/RARE\_PREREG.md}). As an independent reference we used the same donors' Micro-PVM\_2 nuclei (disjoint cells, median 174 per donor). \emph{Calibration gate.} In a simulation with these donors' exact cell numbers, LCP's FDR was 0.075\,(0.004), above the pre-specified tolerance, and its type-I error for strong within-only pairs 0.086; pseudobulk's FDR was 0.549 and its within-only type-I error 0.450. By our rule the real-data comparison below is therefore descriptive: LCP is much less affected than pseudobulk at these cell numbers but is not calibrated. \emph{Divergence.} Pseudobulk called 9{,}970 pairs and LCP 8{,}698; 3{,}926 pseudobulk calls (0.39) were not LCP calls. Their median leak share was 0.13, against 0.05 for pairs called by both, and they replicated as same-sign LCP calls in the deep Micro-PVM\_2 reference less often (0.39) than shared calls (0.63) or LCP-only calls (0.51). \emph{Q1 (are they within-donor programs?): NO.} In the independent Micro-PVM\_2 cells their median absolute within-donor correlation (0.050) was higher than that of LCP-only pairs (0.045) but lower than that of shared calls (0.058), so the pre-specified criterion failed. In Lee 2026 donors they replicated as often as LCP-only pairs (0.42 vs 0.42). \emph{Q2 (does removing them change a downstream result?): YES.} Removing the pseudobulk-only pairs replaced 10 of the top-20 hub genes of the pseudobulk network (Jaccard 0.33; random removal of the same number of edges: median 0.67, 5th percentile 0.48). In the more abundant Micro-PVM\_3 supertype (83 donors, median 43 nuclei) Q1 was NO and Q2 NO (Jaccard 0.67, random 5th percentile 0.60), and the same simulation gate gave LCP FDR 0.051 (0.003) and pseudobulk 0.169 (not in the plan). So with naturally few cells the two methods give materially different networks, and the matched simulation says pseudobulk's version is far less reliable; but we could not show directly on real data that the discarded pairs are within-donor programs.
