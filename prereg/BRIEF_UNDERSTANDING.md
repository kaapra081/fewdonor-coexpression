# Brief understanding (recomb_v2), written Mon 26 Oct 2026, ~09:50 PDT

**Goal.** A RECOMB 2027 main-track methods paper whose primary contribution is new: valid *and* useful inference on between-donor co-expression, Σ_B,jk = Cov_D(E[θ_j | D], E[θ_k | D]) on the latent relative-expression scale with equal donor weights, when donors are few (8–24) or cells per donor are few (~40). It must not repackage the round-3 benchmark (that goes to a journal); it may cite it as a preprint.

**Why the problem is open (round-3 held-out numbers, R = 200).** At 16 donors the only valid test (CMB_mom: count-scale moment correction + donor jackknife) has BH power 0.004; the count-scale donor-mean Pearson without correction (CMB_naive) has power 0.93 but FDR 0.135, and log-of-sums pseudobulk 0.94 at FDR 0.138, because within-donor co-expression leaks into donor means through the finite-cell sampling covariance κ_d·Cov_d(θ). In the rare-cell design (40 donors, ~40 cells) the figures are 0.078 (CMB_mom) versus 0.72 / 0.70 at FDR 0.16 / 0.21. Jackknife 95% intervals cover as little as 0.82. The gap between CMB_naive and CMB_mom says the loss is mostly in the *inference* step (equal weights, raw-covariance scale, a jackknife variance with D − 1 df), not in the bias correction itself.
