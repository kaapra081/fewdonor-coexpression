\section{Planted signal, depth-matched, library-preserving and planted-confounder nulls}\label{app:plan6}

All analyses in this section follow Plan~6 (pre-specified; \texttt{rev5/PLAN6.md}) or its three post hoc follow-ups (Plans 6b--6e, each hashed before its own results; \texttt{rev5/PLAN6B.md}--\texttt{PLAN6E.md}). Code: \texttt{rev5/rev5.py}, \texttt{rev5\_post.py}, \texttt{rev5\_c.py}, \texttt{rev5\_d.py}, \texttt{rev5\_e.py}; results: \texttt{rev5/out/}. Every run used real PsychAD or OneK1K cells.

\paragraph{Planted signal.} Per subtype, genes with pooled mean at least 0.1 UMI per cell, excluding XIST and the seven Y genes, were eligible; 40 were drawn into four modules of ten with latent correlations $\rho=0.1,0.2,0.3,0.4$ (180 true pairs). The between-donor log-SD $s$ was the median over eligible genes of $\hat\tau_j/\hat\mu_j$ from Paule--Mandel on the real donors' ratio means (\SpS). Donor-centred cells were dealt to pseudo-donors with the real cell numbers; pseudo-donor $d$ drew $z_{dk}\sim N(0,1)$ per module and $e_{dj}\sim N(0,1)$ per gene, and every cell of $d$ had its gene-$j$ counts multiplied by $\exp(u_{dj}-s^2/2)$ with $u_{dj}=s(\sqrt{\rho_k}z_{dk}+\sqrt{1-\rho_k}e_{dj})$ for module genes and $u_{dj}=se_{dj}$ otherwise. The true between-donor correlation of a module pair is $(e^{s^2\rho}-1)/(e^{s^2}-1)$. FDR is the mean over 20 runs of the false discovery proportion of BH at 5\% over all $\sim$89{,}000 pairs. At full size pseudobulk made \SpPBcalls{} calls per run and XDS-M \SpXcalls. At 200 donors no calibrated method had useful power (XDS-M \SpXpowTwo; pseudobulk FDR \SpPBfdrTwo).

\paragraph{XDS-r.} $\widehat{\rho}_{jk}=\bar q^{\,\mathrm{sym}}_{jk}/(\bar q_{jj}\bar q_{kk})^{1/2}$, where $\bar q^{\,\mathrm{sym}}_{jk}$ averages $(R^{A_s}_{dj}R^{B_s}_{dk}+R^{A_s}_{dk}R^{B_s}_{dj})/2$ over donors and splits and $\bar q_{jj}$ averages $R^{A_s}_{dj}R^{B_s}_{dj}$ (cross-half reliability); it is undefined if $\bar q_{jj}\le0$ (0.2--1.7\% of evaluated pairs). Because both numerator and denominator are cross-half products, neither the leak nor measurement noise enters their expectations, which is why the estimate is not attenuated. On real donors XDS-r and pseudobulk correlation had Spearman correlation \XrSpear{} across pairs; the pre-specified prediction that $r_{\mathrm{PB}}-\widehat\rho$ would increase with the within-donor correlation $r_w$ was not borne out (Spearman $-0.35$ to $-0.54$), because attenuation of $r_{\mathrm{PB}}$ dominates the comparison.

\paragraph{Depth-matched null and XDS-Mh.} Cells were binned into ten equal-count bins of log library size and donor labels were permuted within bins, so each pseudo-donor keeps its real donor's number of cells per depth bin. The across-donor SD of $\log\hat r_d$, $\hat r_d=\sum_cs_c^2/\sum_cs_c$, was \DmSDthis{} (real donors \DmSDreal{}; plain donor-centred null \DmSDplain). XDS-M made false calls in \DmXany{} of 20 runs (mean \DmXmean{} per run; per-pair type-I \DmXtI); pseudobulk made \DmPB{} per run. Three adjustments were tried, in this order: XDS-Md residualizes both halves' $R$ on the full-donor summaries $[1,\log\hat r_d,\log n_d]$ (pre-specified fallback; it held here, \DmXdany{} runs, but failed in the OneK1K null, \OnXdany{} of 10 runs); XDS-Mda residualizes the half means on the same summaries before standardizing (post hoc; failed in OneK1K, \OnXdaany{} of 10, and at 200 donors); XDS-Mh residualizes each half's $R^h$ on that half's own summaries $[1,\log\hat r^h_d,\log n^h_d]$ (post hoc). The full-donor summaries share information between halves under depth--state coupling, because the depths of half-$A$ cells carry information on half-$A$ states; XDS-Mh does not. XDS-Mh met its rule in every evaluation: depth-matched null \MhDany{} runs (type-I \MhDtI), donor-centred null at full size \MhAany{} and at 200 donors \MhTwoany{} (type-I \MhAlltI), 100 donors \MhHundany{} (descriptive), OneK1K null \MhOany{} of 10, planted signal FDR \MhSfdr{} with power \MhSpow. Earlier evaluations in this paper (small cohorts, brain banks, SEA-AD, covariate nulls) used XDS-M, not XDS-Mh.

\paragraph{Plan 8 (post hoc; \texttt{rev5/PLAN8.md}, SHA-256 \PeSha\ldots, written after an external review and before computation).} XDS-Mh as defined above, on the same reassignments as the earlier plans; XDS-M recomputed on them matched the earlier results in every run (20/20 lupus runs per null and cell type, 40/40 brain-bank runs per subtype, 20/20 planted-signal runs per subtype). \emph{Held-out lupus cohort} (rule: at most 2 of 20 runs with a call and per-pair type-I $\le0.055$): donor-centred null \PeLAh{} runs in monocytes and pDCs (type-I \PeLAhtI; at most \PeLAhFCmax{} call per run), so XDS-Mh held in monocytes and failed in pDCs; library-preserving null \PeLDh{} of 20 runs (mean calls \PeLDhMean), failing as XDS-M did; real monocyte donors \PeLRh{} calls, \PeLRkeep{} of XDS-M's, including \PeLRisg{} interferon-stimulated gene pairs, all positive. \emph{Brain-bank-stratified null} (Plan 5 R2 reassignments; rule: at most 4 of 40 runs and type-I $\le0.055$): XDS-Mh \PeBh{} (type-I \PeBhtI), XDS-Mhb, which adds the pseudo-donor's bank indicators to each half's columns, \PeBhb{} (\PeBhbtI); by the interpretation fixed in the plan, known batch indicators should be added and XDS-Mh alone does not handle bank structure. \emph{Power at matched achieved FDR} (planted signal, 20 runs per subtype): along each method's $p$-value ordering, the longest prefix whose false-discovery proportion is at most 0.05 contained \PeMatchPB{} of the planted pairs for pseudobulk, \PeMatchKX{} for KX, \PeMatchXlist{} for XDS-M and \PeMatchHlist{} for XDS-Mh (SD over runs at most \PeMatchSDmax; at 0.10: \PeMatchTenPB, \PeMatchTenKX, \PeMatchTenX, \PeMatchTenH). \paragraph{Library-preserving null.} Most PsychAD donors were sequenced in two multiplexed libraries (\texttt{poolID}; about four donors per library). Cells were centred by exact donor-mean removal and donor labels were permuted within each library. XDS-M made false calls in \LnXany{} of 20 runs (mean \LnXmean), and so did splitting by library. This null is confounded by its own construction: exact centring fixes each donor's total, so disjoint subsets of one donor's cells have negatively correlated means, and within-library permutation puts many cells of one donor into both halves of a pseudo-donor. As predicted, \LnOpp\% of XDS-M's false calls had sign opposite to $r_w$ (expected by chance \LnOppBase\%) in P1, P2, P3; the exception, P1, means the artifact does not explain everything. Splitting by source donor, which removes the artifact, still left false calls in \LnSrcAny{} of 20 runs (mean \LnSrcMean), so library-level effects are not excluded. On real donors, splitting by library instead of at random kept \LibKeep{} of the calls of an equal-power single random split.

\paragraph{Planted confounder and XDS-Mca.} Pseudo-donors carried the covariates of the real donor whose label they received; 200 eligible genes were multiplied by 1.2 in AD cases. XDS-M called \CfXplanted{} planted pairs per run, as it should (the pairs are associated marginally). XDS-Mc, which residualizes the standardized $R^h$ on the covariates, made false calls in \CfXcany{} of 20 runs (type-I among planted pairs \CfXctI); covariate-adjusted pseudobulk made \CfPBc. The reason is that standardization turns a multiplicative effect $\delta\mu_j$ into $\delta\mu_j(v^{*h}_{dj})^{-1/2}$, which varies with the donor's design, and its residual after regression is shared by all affected genes. XDS-Mca regresses each half's ratio means $m^h_{dj}$ on the covariates first and divides by $(v^{*h}_{dj})^{1/2}$ second; its mean is zero under the conditional null whatever the design. It made false calls in \CfXcaany{} of 20 runs (AD-only adjustment \CfXcaADany; type-I \CfXcatI) and kept \CaKeep{} of XDS-M's real-donor calls. XDS-Mca combined with XDS-Mh (Plan 6e) held at run level (\HcCany{} of 20 in the confounder null) but its per-pair type-I was \HcTI{} across the depth-matched and donor-centred nulls, above the bound; the combination is not recommended without a degrees-of-freedom correction, which we did not test.

\paragraph{Sex control.} Expression sex disagreed with recorded sex for \SexMismatch{} donors. Within men and within women separately, XDS-M called XIST--Y pairs in \SexWithinXY{} (men/women) of 7, so the XIST--Y association that survived XDS-Mc came from pooling the sexes after standardization; under XDS-Mca \CaXISTY{} of 7 were called. Y--Y pairs were called within men in \SexWithinMaleYY{} of 21, and the men's mean standardized Y score fell with age (Spearman \SexLOY), consistent with age-related mosaic loss of chromosome Y in microglia; within women, where Y genes are barely expressed, \SexWithinFemaleYY{} of 21 were called, which we cannot explain and suspect reflects ambient RNA from male donors in the same library. Removing discordant nuclei did not change these conclusions (\texttt{rev5/out/post\_R\_*.json}).

\paragraph{OneK1K real donors.} Pseudobulk made \OnekRealPB{} calls and XDS-M \OnekRealX{} in B1--B3.

\paragraph{Lupus cohort (Plan 7).} Classical monocytes (LM; 307{,}429 cells, 261 donors, median 1{,}005 per donor) and plasmacytoid dendritic cells (pDC; 5{,}181 cells, 235 donors, median 15), 447 genes (99{,}681 pairs) each; 20 reassignments per null. The 88 libraries form 24 pools of libraries with identical donor sets, inferred from library--donor incidence; processing cohort 1 has no SLE donor, and SLE donors had more monocytes and fewer pDCs than controls. Plan, amendments, code and every result: \texttt{lupus/}.
