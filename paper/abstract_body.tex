In multi-donor single-cell RNA-seq, two genes can co-vary within donors, reflecting cell-state programs, or between donors, reflecting genetic, environmental, disease or technical variation. Between-donor co-expression is usually tested by correlating pseudobulk donor means. With cells sampled independently within donors, the covariance of donor ratio-means is the between-donor covariance plus the within-donor covariance scaled by each donor's effective inverse cell count. This is a count-scale instance of the classical multilevel (BCES-type) measurement-error decomposition, and it means that cell-state programs leak into pseudobulk. We estimate the leak for each donor from count moments. In a Gaussian working model with known sampling variances and no leak, a test that uses only the two genes' donor means has at most the local power of an oracle with $D_{\mathrm{eff}}=\sum_d\rho_{dj}\rho_{dk}$ perfectly measured donors, where $\rho$ is the reliability of a donor mean. For the 417 genes we analysed in SEA-AD and Lee et al. 2026 microglia, the median $D_{\mathrm{eff}}/D$ is 0.90--0.98 at the cohorts' cell numbers but 0.53 at 40 cells per donor. We propose LCP, a leak-corrected, precision-standardized donor-permutation test of independence of donor effects. In simulations with exact truth and about 40 cells per donor, LCP's false discovery rate (FDR) was 0.046--0.050 at 24--40 donors, against 0.13--0.14 for pseudobulk correlation. In five held-out designs frozen after development (24--40 donors), it was 0.041--0.050. Post hoc ablations show that standardization and permutation remove most of pseudobulk's FDR excess, while the leak term keeps the type-I error for within-donor-only pairs near 0.05 (0.11--0.21 without it) and is needed for FDR control at 40 donors (0.058 without it). Power is low with few cells (0.11--0.12 at 24 donors with about 40 cells each) and matches a noise-matched oracle, not the information bound. LCP is not calibrated at 16 donors with 40 cells (FDR 0.089), when sequencing depth is coupled to cell state (0.22 in a stress design; a post hoc check finds within-donor depth--expression correlations of similar size in both cohorts), or when donor effects are dependent but uncorrelated (type-I error 0.06--0.10 at 0.05). Covariance intervals undercover even on true donor means. In a naturally rare SEA-AD supertype (79 donors, median 15 nuclei), 39\% of pseudobulk calls are not LCP calls and removing them changes half of the network's top-20 hub genes; a simulation matched to those cell numbers gives pseudobulk an FDR of 0.55 and LCP 0.075, so LCP is far less affected but not calibrated there, and we could not show directly that the discarded pairs are within-donor programs. At the cohorts' real cell numbers the estimated leak is under 1\% of the pseudobulk covariance among pseudobulk calls, and LCP and pseudobulk largely agree.
